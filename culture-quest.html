Charles Handy — Gods of Management

Four different ways an organisation can distribute power, organise work and value people. Most organisations can contain a mix; no type is automatically best in every situation.

⚡
ZEUS · POWER / CLUBInfluence is concentrated around a central person or small inner circle. Fast, personal decisions.
🏛️
APOLLO · ROLEAuthority comes from positions, responsibilities, rules and procedures. Order and predictability.
🧠
ATHENA · TASKExpertise and problem-solving matter. Flexible teams form around tasks and outcomes.
🎭
DIONYSUS · PERSONThe organisation supports independent professionals. Autonomy and individual purpose matter.
