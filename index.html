Recruitment Role-Play Generator

Three unusual jobs. Random roles. One recruitment challenge.

How to play

1. Enter namesPaste each class list below, with one student name per line.
2. Generate teamsThe generator randomly selects 3 recruiters, one for each job. Everyone else becomes an applicant.
3. Prepare and interviewRecruiters create an advert. Applicants prepare a CV. Then conduct the interview.

Each case gets one recruiter. Applicants are distributed as evenly as possible. Every person in a case receives the same HR package.

Student task checklist

Recruiter

  • Create a job advertisement and person specification.
  • Include the assigned recruitment method and HR package.
  • Prepare three interview questions.
  • Interview every applicant assigned to your case.

Applicant

  • Create a professional CV/resume for the assigned job.
  • Show skills and experience that match the person specification.
  • Prepare three questions to ask the recruiter.
  • Use the assigned HR package to decide what to ask about the job.
